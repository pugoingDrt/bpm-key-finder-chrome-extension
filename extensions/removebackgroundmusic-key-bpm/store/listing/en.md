## Description (paste into the store)

BPM & Key Finder shows the tempo and musical key of the tracks on your computer in seconds. Drop in an MP3, WAV, M4A, FLAC or OGG file, or even a video, and you get:

• The key, for example "A minor", with its relative major or minor
• Its Camelot code and Open Key notation, for harmonic mixing
• The BPM, plus half and double time
• Compatible keys on the Camelot wheel, for smooth DJ transitions

ANALYZE A WHOLE BATCH
Drop several tracks, or a whole folder, at once. They are analyzed one after another, and you can copy the results or download them as a CSV file for your DJ library or set notes.

TAP TEMPO BUILT IN
No file at hand? Tap the button along with the beat, or press T, and read the tempo as you go.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Everything runs in your browser. Your files are never uploaded and nothing is stored. The extension asks for no permissions, so it cannot read the websites you visit.

WHO IT'S FOR
DJs preparing sets, producers picking samples that fit, singers and musicians working out what to play, and anyone making remixes or mashups or practicing along to music.

HOW TO USE IT
1. Click the extension icon to open it in a tab.
2. Drop tracks onto the page, or click to choose files.
3. Read the results, or copy them.

Tracks up to 10 minutes are supported. Results are an estimate from the audio: for music with tempo changes or unusual harmony, check them by ear.

Camelot and Open Key are notations used by DJ software; this extension is not affiliated with their owners.
